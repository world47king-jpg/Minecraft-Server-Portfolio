FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about my services and process

What technologies do you use? +

I work with a wide range of modern technologies including HTML/CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, and various databases. For Minecraft servers, I use Java, Spigot/Paper API, and custom plugin development. I always choose the best tools for each specific project.

How long does a typical project take? +

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A simple website might take 1-2 weeks, while a complex web application or Minecraft server setup could take 4-8 weeks. I always provide detailed timelines before starting any project and keep you updated throughout the process.

Do you offer ongoing support? +

Yes! I offer ongoing maintenance and support packages for all my projects. This includes regular updates, security patches, performance monitoring, and technical support. I'm committed to ensuring your digital products continue to run smoothly long after launch.

Can you work with my existing team? +

Absolutely! I'm experienced in collaborating with existing teams and can seamlessly integrate into your workflow. Whether you need me to lead a project or work as part of a team, I'm flexible and adaptable to your needs and processes.
