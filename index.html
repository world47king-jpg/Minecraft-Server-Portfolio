FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to frequently asked questions about my services

What is your typical project timeline? +

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A simple website typically takes 2-4 weeks, while more complex applications can take 2-3 months. I provide detailed timelines during the initial consultation and keep you updated throughout the project.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance? +

Yes! I offer various maintenance packages to ensure your website or server continues to run smoothly. This includes security updates, performance optimization, bug fixes, and feature enhancements as needed.

Can you help with Minecraft server optimization? +

Absolutely! I specialize in Minecraft server optimization, including plugin configuration, hardware tuning, network optimization, and custom plugin development to reduce lag and improve player experience.

What technologies do you work with? +

I work with a wide range of technologies including HTML/CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Python, and various databases. For Minecraft, I work with Java, Spigot/Paper, and various server management tools. I'm always learning new technologies to stay current.

How do you handle project pricing? +

I offer both fixed-price and hourly rate options depending on the project type. After discussing your requirements, I provide a detailed quote with no hidden costs. I believe in transparent pricing and will always discuss any changes before proceeding.
